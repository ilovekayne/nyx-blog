## 11:00 PM

The lab is still pretty full. Someone has music going. Someone else is fighting a VM that won't boot.

Kayne is walking between desks, asking how everyone's doing.

## 1:00 AM

Half the room is gone. The VM still won't boot. Kayne pulls up a chair.

"Okay. Walk me through what you've tried."

## 3:00 AM

The VM boots.

Turns out it was a typo in a config file. Kayne found it by asking questions until the person who wrote it found it themselves.

## 3:05 AM

Kayne packs up, says "nice work tonight," and leaves like it was nothing.

It wasn't nothing. It never is.
