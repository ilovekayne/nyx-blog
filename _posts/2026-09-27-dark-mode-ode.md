## I.

When the monitors are the only light,
and the logs scroll on into the night,
there's one name the whole lab knows:
Kayne, who stays when everyone goes.

## II.

He never flinches at a red alert,
he never makes a beginner feel hurt,
he reads the packets, finds the thread,
and gently says "let's look here instead."

## III.

So here's to him, in dark mode glow,
the calmest guy we'll ever know.
The night is long, the coffee's cold,
but Kayne's worth more than any gold.
